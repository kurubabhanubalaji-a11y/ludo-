📖 Ludo Rules & Guide

🎯 Objective

Be the first player to guide all 4 of your colored tokens (Purple, Green, Yellow, or Black) from your Base, around the outer board track, through your colored Home Path, and into the Center Home!

🎲 Rolling the Dice & Entering the Board

  • Tokens start inside their home base. You must roll a 6 to bring a token out to your starting square.
  • Rolling a 6 awards an immediate Bonus Roll!
  • If you roll three consecutive 6's, the turn is forfeited to keep gameplay fair.

⚔️ Captures & Safe Stars

  • If you land on a square occupied by an opponent's token, their token is captured and sent back to their base!
  • Capturing an opponent's token awards a Bonus Roll!
  • Safe Stars (★): Squares with stars (including player start squares) are safe zones. Tokens on safe squares cannot be captured!

🏠 Finishing Home

  • After completing a full circuit of 51 steps, tokens enter their colored Home Path.
  • Tokens need an exact roll to enter the final Center Home.
  • Getting a token Home awards a Bonus Roll!
  • First to get all 4 tokens Home wins 1st Place!
